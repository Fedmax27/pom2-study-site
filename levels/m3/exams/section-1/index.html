Section 1 Exam

A General Approach to the Undifferentiated Patient · 30 questions · multiple choice & fill-in-the-blank

This exam draws a fresh, randomized 30-question set every attempt from a larger local question bank covering every learning objective from both lessons in this section. The correct answer is revealed right after you answer each question, and the score tracker at the top updates as you go.

This runs entirely in your browser: no API key, no network calls. Questions are drawn and shuffled from a hand-written bank rather than generated live by a model, and fill-in-the-blank grading matches your typed answer against a short list of accepted answers locally rather than true semantic understanding. To upgrade to live AI-generated questions and grading, replace buildExamSet() and gradeBlank() in assets/js/exam.js with calls to your own backend.
