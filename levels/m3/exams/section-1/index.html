Section 1 Exam

A General Approach to the Undifferentiated Patient · 30 questions · multiple choice & short answer

This exam draws a fresh, randomized 30-question set every attempt from a larger local question bank covering every learning objective from both lessons in this section. Short-answer questions are graded on whether your answer hits the key concepts — exact wording doesn't matter.

This runs entirely in your browser: no API key, no network calls. Questions are drawn and shuffled from a hand-written bank rather than generated live by a model, and short-answer grading matches key concepts locally rather than true semantic understanding. To upgrade to live AI-generated questions and grading, replace buildExamSet() and gradeFreeText() in assets/js/exam.js with calls to your own backend.
