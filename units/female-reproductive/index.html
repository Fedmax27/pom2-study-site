Female Reproductive

The reproductive block: family planning, fertility, the drugs used in gynecology, and the common gynecologic presentations. The anatomy, embryology and hormone axes behind it all are in Endocrinology Week 3.

Week 4

Family Planning, Gynecology & Prenatal Screening

  • Abortion, infertility, contraception and gynecologic drugs; discharge, STIs, cervical screening and early pregnancy; bleeding, the vulva and pelvic pain; and prenatal genetic screening →
Week 5

Menopause, the Pelvic Mass, the Breast & Pregnancy

  • Post-menopausal bleeding, the pelvic mass, menopause, D&C and hysterectomy; breast pathology and common breast problems; early pregnancy — care providers, teratogens, first-trimester ultrasound and bleeding; then first-trimester and antenatal care, sexual health, the anatomy scan and hypertension in pregnancy →
Week 6

Preterm Birth, Bleeding & Labour

  • Preterm labour and PPROM, the small fetus, and antepartum hemorrhage; then labour itself — the normal process and fetal monitoring, induction, forceps and vacuum, caesarean and TOLAC, postpartum hemorrhage, and hemorrhagic shock; then the first weeks after birth — neonatal care, postpartum care and lactation →
Later weeks will be added as they're covered.
