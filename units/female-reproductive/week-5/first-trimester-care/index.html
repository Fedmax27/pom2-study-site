    Care for Pregnancy in the First Trimester

    If You Remember Nothing Else
    • Missed menses is how most pregnancies are found — but with irregular cycles, or spotting mistaken for a period, the symptoms carry the diagnosis.
    • Serum β-hCG: > 25 is positive, < 5 negative. About 1500 before a transvaginal scan shows an intrauterine sac, about 5000 transabdominally. A pregnancy is confirmed in the uterus only when you see a gestational sac and a yolk sac.
    • Date by the first ultrasound, not the LMP — any scan before 23 weeks wins. Crown–rump length until 14 weeks; IVF transfer date beats everything. Once set, the due date never moves — a later scan that disagrees is telling you about growth.
    • GTPAL counts pregnancies, not babies — except L, which counts living children. Twins are one pregnancy.
    • Fundus at the symphysis at 12 weeks, at the umbilicus at 20; from 20 weeks the symphysis–fundal height in cm ≈ weeks.
    • Everyone gets: CBC, group & screen, rubella, hepatitis B, HIV, syphilis, GC/chlamydia and a urine culture — and is offered eFTS at 11–14 weeks. HSV is not routine.

    Diagnosing Pregnancy

    SituationHow pregnancy usually announces itself
    Regular cycles, trying to conceiveMissed menses (amenorrhea) — typically the first sign. Classic early symptoms: nausea/vomiting, urinary frequency, breast tenderness, and later in the trimester abdominal distension and bloating
    Irregular cycles, or not tryingSymptoms matter more, because months without a period may be normal for her. Some present only once they feel fetal movement
    Altered bleedingIrregular spotting or a lighter-than-usual “period” can be early implantation bleeding — especially at the extremes of age and in women on the pill. Heavier early-pregnancy bleeding is easily mistaken for menses and delays the diagnosis

    Confirming it

    Urine hCG (over the counter, often done before she sees anyone) → serum hCG when you need certainty → ultrasound for a viable intrauterine pregnancy. Maternal, then external, perception of fetal activity also confirms it — sometimes months in.

    Serum β-hCG (IU/L)What it means
    < 5Negative
    5–25Grey zone — a very early pregnancy, or within the lab's error
    > 25Typically diagnostic of pregnancy
    ~1500An intrauterine gestation is usually visible on transvaginal ultrasound
    ~5000Usually visible on transabdominal ultrasound — lower in a very thin woman, higher with a high BMI
    Where This Picks Up Thursday's Lecture

    Dr. Schmidt gives the classic 1500 threshold. The first-trimester ultrasound lecture argues the discriminatory zone should be raised to 2000–3000, because a normal pregnancy is sometimes not yet visible at 1500 — see 5.10. Treat 1500 as “usually visible”, not “must be visible”.

    Dating the Pregnancy

    Set the due date as early as possible. It is what every later decision hangs on — above all counselling at the peri-viable 23–24 weeks and managing post-dates at 41–42 weeks. Human gestation is ~280 days = 40 completed weeks from the last normal menstrual period (LNMP).

    MethodHowRank
    IVF embryo transfer dateConception time is known exactlyOverrides everything, including later ultrasound
    First-trimester ultrasoundCrown–rump length (CRL) up to 14 weeks. If several early scans, use the first with CRL ≥ 10 mm (~7 weeks)The standard for everyone else — any ultrasound before 23 weeks is used instead of the LNMP (SOGC Guideline #303, 2014)
    Second-trimester ultrasoundAfter 14 weeks CRL is unreliable (flexion and orientation vary), so use a composite: head circumference, biparietal diameter, abdominal circumference, femur lengthUsed if no earlier scan
    Naegele's ruleLNMP + 7 days − 3 months + 1 year. In practice a pregnancy wheel or online/app calculatorA rough estimate only

    Naegele's worked example: LNMP 10 January 2026 → +7 days = 17 January → −3 months = 17 October → +1 year = EDB 17 October 2026 (exactly 280 days).

    Why the Guideline Stopped “Keeping the LMP If It Fits”

    The old habit was to keep the LNMP date if it fell within the first scan's range of error. SOGC #303 looked at the dating evidence and found the early ultrasound is simply the most accurate estimate — even when it differs by only a day or two. So the first-trimester scan sets the date. After that the EDB is fixed. A later scan measuring “ahead” or “behind” means the fetus is larger or smaller than expected — a growth question, not a new due date. The one caveat he gives: every test has error, including a mis-transcribed report, so if a scan clearly contradicts everything else, clinical judgement can set it aside.

    G — GravidaTotal pregnancies, including the current one—
    T — TermPregnancies delivered at ≥ 37 weeksTwins at term = one T
    P — PretermPregnancies delivered at 20 to 36+6 weeksA stillbirth still counts here or in T

    G/P is quick and favoured in family medicine and uncomplicated histories (“G7 P6”); GTPAL is preferred because it shows the preterm births and losses that make a pregnancy higher-risk.

    The First 12 Weeks: Symptoms and Uterine Growth

    Symptoms are variable — some women have none, others blame them on something they ate — so they help but never confirm a pregnancy on their own.

    First trimesterWhyLater trimesters (if found late)
    Amenorrhea or irregular spottinghCG keeps the corpus luteum going, so the hormone drop that triggers menses never comesAmenorrhea finally noticed — especially with prior irregular cycles
    Nausea/vomiting, food aversionsRapidly rising hCGNausea/vomiting
    Frequent urinationRising blood volume and GFR, pressure from the uterus, smooth-muscle relaxationWorse frequency, even incontinence
    Breast tenderness · fatigue · mood swingsHormonal stimulation; metabolic demandAbdominal bloating · fetal movements · heartburn

    It is urinary frequency, not urinary retention, that goes with early pregnancy — the module's own check turns on that word.

    WeeksUterusWhere the fundus is
    Non-pregnantAbout her closed fistEntirely in the pelvis

    14–20A small/medium cantaloupe; now an abdominal organRising, with lower abdominal distension

    20–26Fundus tips forward towards the abdominal wallOrgan displacement increases

    First-Trimester Investigations

    A confirmatory hCG isn't always needed, but is useful with irregular cycles or a history of recurrent miscarriage or ectopic. Once the pregnancy is confirmed, order the baseline:

    Recommended for every pregnancyWhy
    CBCHemoglobin, MCV (a low MCV prompts hemoglobinopathy screening) and platelets
    Group & screenBlood type, Rh status, and any red-cell antibodies (see 5.14)
    RubellaImmunity. If non-immune, vaccinate postpartum — it is a live vaccine
    Hepatitis B, HIV, syphilis (VDRL)Treatments exist that reduce harm to her and the baby
    Gonorrhea / chlamydiaVaginal swab or urine
    Urine cultureAsymptomatic bacteriuria — smooth-muscle relaxation means poorer bladder emptying and more infection
    For selected womenWhen
    TSHKnown hypo- or hyperthyroidism — the dose often needs adjusting
    Hepatitis C, HSV, other STIsHigh-risk population or known exposure. HSV is not routine: antibodies alone don't predict transmission; test for an active genital lesion near delivery
    Hemoglobinopathy screenMediterranean, Southeast Asian or African ancestry (sickle cell), or a low MCV/Hb
    NIPT or CVSA known carrier of a testable condition (balanced translocation, muscular dystrophy…)
    eFTS — Offered to Everyone, at 11–14 Weeks

    The enhanced first trimester screen combines a nuchal translucency ultrasound with four serum analytes, adjusted for maternal age, ethnicity and gestation, to estimate the risk of trisomy 21 and other chromosomal problems. It is offered regardless of age or risk factors, because any pregnancy can be affected. Declining it still leaves later options. The screening menu in full is in 4.16.

    Why the Thyroid Dose Changes

    The lecture's reasoning is that hCG resembles TSH (the audio says “thyroid hormone”, but the structural match is with TSH). hCG weakly stimulates the thyroid, so TSH can dip in the first trimester, and levothyroxine requirements usually rise 25–50% in pregnancy. That is why a woman with known thyroid disease gets a TSH early.
