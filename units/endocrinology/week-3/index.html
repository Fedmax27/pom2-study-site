Week 3

The week starts with the two glands that run the endocrine system from the top down, the adrenal and the pituitary: too little, then too much, one hormone at a time. Then puberty and growth, and the reproductive tract itself: its anatomy, how it develops, what happens when sex development is atypical, gender-affirming care, and PMOS. Each lecture is one page, however many decks it came in.
