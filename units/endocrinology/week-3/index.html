Week 3

The two glands that run the endocrine system from the top down — the adrenal and the pituitary. Each lecture runs as four decks and each is gathered here as the single lecture it was given as: too little, then too much, one hormone at a time.

3.1

The Adrenal Gland: Insufficiency & Overproduction

  • Adrenal physiology and the HPA axis, primary vs secondary insufficiency and the adrenal crisis, primary hyperaldosteronism and the aldosterone:renin ratio, pheochromocytoma and the incidentaloma, and Cushing syndrome→
3.2

The Pituitary Gland: Insufficiency & Overproduction

  • The hypothalamic–pituitary axes, mass effect and bitemporal hemianopia, the inappropriately normal TSH, treating adrenal before thyroid, and overproduction of cortisol, prolactin and growth hormone→
3.3

Physiology of Puberty & the Menstrual Cycle

  • Part 1 — the axis across a lifetime, the GABA brake and the kisspeptin trigger, critical mass and leptin, and disorders of pubertal timing at each level→
  • Part 2 — the HPO feedback loops, FSH and LH in the ovary, the cycle phase by phase, dysmenorrhea, PMS/PMDD and menopause→
3.4

Abnormalities of Puberty

  • Normal sequence and Tanner staging, delayed puberty — constitutional delay, Turner and Klinefelter, primary vs secondary hypogonadism — and precocious puberty: the benign variants, central vs peripheral, McCune–Albright and GnRH analogue treatment→
