Musculoskeletal

The MSK block: anatomy built around clinical problems, region by region. It starts at the spine and the shoulder, then works out to the rest of the limbs and the rheumatologic diseases.

Week 7

The Spine, Back & Neck Pain, and the Shoulder

  • The MSK concept reference (terms, the somatic nerve, dermatomes, contractions, joints and the skeleton); mechanical and non-mechanical back and neck pain with their red flags; reading spine x-rays; and the bones and joints of the shoulder, from the AC joint to frozen shoulder →
Week 8

The Upper Limb, Shoulder to Hand

  • The brachial plexus; the muscles, vessels and nerves of the shoulder, arm and forearm; the bones of the elbow, wrist and hand; rotator cuff disease, biceps tendinopathy, elbow dislocation, nursemaid's elbow, UCL injury, bursitis, tennis and golfer's elbow, and ulnar neuropathy; and distal radius and scaphoid fractures →
Week 9

Finishing the Arm; the Hip, Knee and Leg

  • Upper limb x-rays; the nerves of the hand, carpal tunnel syndrome and ulnar neuropathy at the wrist; the hip joint, its blood supply and hip fractures; the gluteal region and the three compartments of the thigh; hip pain by location; the knee's ligaments, menisci and their injuries; the four compartments of the leg; anterior knee pain, injections and the approach to knee pain →
Week 10

The Foot and Ankle, Nerve Injury, Fractures and Pain

  • Hip, knee and ankle x-rays; grading and localizing nerve injuries and what an EMG shows; the ankle and foot's joints, ligaments, arches and pulses, with sprains and the Ottawa rules, Achilles and plantar fascia problems, tibialis posterior dysfunction, bunions, Morton's neuroma and the diabetic foot (Monday so far) →
Later weeks will be added as they're covered.
