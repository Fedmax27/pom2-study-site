Week 10

The lower limb finishes and the clinical topics take over. Monday reads hip, knee and ankle x-rays; grades and localizes nerve injuries (neurapraxia to neurotmesis, root vs nerve, what an EMG can tell you); and works through the ankle and foot: the joints, ligaments, arches and pulses, then sprains and the Ottawa rules, Achilles and plantar fascia problems, tibialis posterior dysfunction, bunions, Morton's neuroma and the diabetic foot. The rest of the week (MSK oncology, fracture management and radiology, chronic pain, physical activity prescription and pediatric orthopedics) will be added as it's covered.

Monday

Nov 2 Lower limb x-rays, nerve injury, then the foot and ankle.
10.1

Lower Limb Radiology

  • The systematic read and TIMS-C; the hip series, Shenton's line and the AP pelvis lines, femoral neck fracture, arthroplasty and OA; the knee's AP, lateral and skyline views, Blumensaat's line, effusions and lipohemarthrosis, bipartite patella, TKA and ACL reconstruction; the ankle mortise, Kager's fat pad, malleolar fractures, spurs and enthesophytes→
10.2

Nerve Injury

  • Nerve layers; neurapraxia, axonotmesis and neurotmesis, Wallerian degeneration and 1 mm a day; radiculopathy vs mononeuropathy, dermatomes and myotomes, the numb thumb (carpal tunnel or C6); what nerve conduction and needle EMG can and can't show; foot drop at the fibular head vs L5; the three practice questions→
10.3

Clinical Approach to the Foot & Ankle

  • Tendons of the dorsum and sole; the talocrural, subtalar and transverse tarsal joints; the lateral and deltoid ligaments and the spring ligament; the arches and weight bearing; arteries, pulses and the foot's nerves; Achilles tendinopathy and rupture, plantar fasciitis, ankle sprains, the Ottawa rules and malleolar fractures, tibialis posterior dysfunction, bunions, Morton's neuroma and the diabetic foot→
